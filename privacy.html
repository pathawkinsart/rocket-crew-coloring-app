Privacy Policy

Rocket Crew Adventures — Coloring App
Last updated: October 11, 2026

Short version: this app collects nothing. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no cookies, and no tracking of any kind. Everything a child creates stays on their own device.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not ask for a name, email address, or any personal information. It does not use analytics, advertising networks, or third-party trackers.

Children's privacy

This app is designed to be safe for children of all ages, including children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, there is nothing to share, sell, or disclose — to us or to anyone else.

Data storage

Coloring pages and any creations are stored only in the browser on the device being used. Nothing is uploaded to a server. If the browser's site data is cleared, local creations are removed.

Offline use

After the first load, the app can run fully offline. Offline use involves no network communication at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "last updated" date.
